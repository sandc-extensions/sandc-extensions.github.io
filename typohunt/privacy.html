Typo Hunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Typo Hunt is a Figma plugin that checks the spelling of text layers in a Figma file and lets you fix typos in place. Spell checking happens entirely inside Figma, using a dictionary built into the plugin.

What we collect

Nothing. Typo Hunt does not collect, transmit, sell or share your files, file contents, personal information or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers, and it is set to make no network requests at all.

What is stored, and where

Typo Hunt reads text in the open file only while you use it, and changes the file only when you choose a fix. Words you add to your dictionary are saved in Figma's local plugin storage on your device so they stay accepted next time. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Payments

Typo Hunt Pro is sold through Figma's own checkout. Figma processes payments; we never see or store your payment details. Figma's privacy policy covers that process.
