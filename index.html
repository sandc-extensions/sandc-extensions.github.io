Browser Extensions

Small, private Chrome extensions by Simple & Clean Digital Products, LLC. None of them collect your data.

  • Hushplay: stops video and audio from playing on their own. Privacy policy
  • LinkScrub: removes tracking from links. Privacy policy
  • Hushping: stops sites asking to send notifications. Privacy policy
  • Leftovers (Figma plugin): cleans up library leftovers in Figma files. Privacy policy
  • Bindery (Figma plugin): binds hard-coded values to variables. Privacy policy
  • Typo Hunt (Figma plugin): spell checks every text layer. Privacy policy
