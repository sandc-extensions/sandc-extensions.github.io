Bindery Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Bindery is a Figma plugin that finds hard-coded colors and numbers in a Figma file that match your variables, and binds them to those variables. It runs entirely inside Figma.

What we collect

Nothing. Bindery does not collect, transmit, sell or share your files, file contents, personal information or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers, and it is set to make no network requests at all.

What is stored, and where

Bindery reads the open file only while you use it, and changes the file only when you choose to bind values. If you choose to match variables from a team library, Figma imports those variables into the file, as it does when you use them yourself. To remember your scan settings and count free-plan binds, Bindery saves your last settings and the number of binds used that day in Figma's local plugin storage on your device. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Payments

Bindery Pro is sold through Figma's own checkout. Figma processes payments; we never see or store your payment details. Figma's privacy policy covers that process.
