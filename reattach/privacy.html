Reattach Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Reattach is a Figma plugin that finds frames detached from a component and turns them back into instances of that component. It runs entirely inside Figma.

What we collect

Nothing. Reattach does not collect, transmit, sell or share your files, file contents, personal information or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers, and it is set to make no network requests at all.

What is stored, and where

Reattach reads the open file only while you use it, and changes the file only when you choose to reattach. If an original component comes from a team library, Figma imports it into the file, as it does when you place it yourself. To count free-plan use, Reattach saves the number of components reattached that day in Figma's local plugin storage on your device. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Payments

Reattach Pro is sold through Figma's own checkout. Figma processes payments; we never see or store your payment details. Figma's privacy policy covers that process.
