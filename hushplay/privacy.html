Hushplay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Hushplay is a Chrome extension that stops video and audio from playing automatically. It is built to work entirely inside your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Hushplay does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, page content or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers.

What is stored, and where

The only data Hushplay keeps is your allowed-sites list and your on/off preferences. These settings are stored with Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync, so they follow your Chrome profile if you use Chrome Sync. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Hushplay asks for access to the websites you visit only so it can do its one job on those pages. It does not read or record what you do there.

Changes
