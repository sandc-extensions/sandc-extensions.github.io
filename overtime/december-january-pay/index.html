Week Ends in December, Paid in January: Which W-2?

The pay date decides. Overtime is reported on the W-2 for the year it is paid, even if the work was done the year before.

A W-2 generally reports pay in the calendar year it was paid. A workweek ending December 26, 2026 and paid on January 1, 2027 goes on the 2027 W-2, including its code TT amount.

Worked example

An employee works 50 hours at $20 in the week ending Saturday, December 26, 2026. Payday is Friday, January 1, 2027.

2026 W-22027 W-2
Qualified overtime for that week (½ × $20 × 10 hours)$0.00$100.00

It's the same rule as regular wages. The employee's 2026 deduction is based on the 2026 W-2, so getting the year right matters for both of you.

Bonuses paid later work the same way

If a bonus for 2026 work is paid in 2027, the extra overtime it creates also goes on the 2027 W-2. See how to calculate code TT with a bonus.

How the calculators handle it

The batch calculator and the spreadsheet both use each week's pay date to decide the year. If a pay date is missing, the week ending date is used instead. Weeks ending after December 20 with no pay date are flagged, so a January payday isn't missed.

If a W-2 already went out with the wrong amount, the IRS says to issue a corrected Form W-2c (IRS FS-2026-13).

Questions

Which W-2 gets overtime for a week paid in January?

Generally the W-2 for the year it was paid. A week worked in December and paid in January is reported on the next year's W-2.

What if I already issued the W-2 with the wrong year?

Issue a corrected Form W-2c to the employee and the Social Security Administration as soon as possible.
