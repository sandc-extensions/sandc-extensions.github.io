Heft Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Heft is a Figma plugin that finds what makes a Figma file heavy, such as oversized images, hidden layers and heavy effects, and fixes it when you choose to. It runs entirely inside Figma.

What we collect

Nothing. Heft does not collect, transmit, sell or share your files, file contents, personal information or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers, and it is set to make no network requests at all.

What is stored, and where

Heft reads the open file only while you use it, and changes the file only when you choose a fix. When it shrinks an image, the image is resized inside the plugin window on your device and placed back into your file; it is never uploaded. To count free-plan use, it saves the number of fixes made that day in Figma's local plugin storage on your device. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Payments

Heft Pro is sold through Figma's own checkout. Figma processes payments; we never see or store your payment details. Figma's privacy policy covers that process.
