How to remove tracking from links in Chrome (utm, fbclid, gclid)

Links from emails, social media and ads carry tracking codes, the utm_source, fbclid and gclid junk on the end of the address. They follow you to the page and come along when you share the link.

The fix: LinkScrub

LinkScrub strips more than 100 kinds of tracking codes from links before the page loads, and adds "Copy clean link" to the right-click menu so the links you share are clean too. It leaves the parts a page needs, like search terms, alone.

Add to ChromeLinkScrub on the Chrome Web Store

How to use it

  1. Add LinkScrub to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open links as usual. Tracking codes are removed automatically.
  3. Right-click any link and choose "Copy clean link" to share it without the tracking.

Price

Free.

Questions

Will it break sites?

It removes only known tracking codes. If a site misbehaves, exclude it in the settings.

What does it collect?

Nothing. It uses Chrome's built-in rule engine and never reads your browsing.
