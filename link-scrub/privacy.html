LinkScrub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

LinkScrub is a Chrome extension that removes tracking parameters from links. It is built to work entirely inside your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. LinkScrub does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, page content or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers.

What is stored, and where

The only data LinkScrub keeps is your excluded-sites list, custom parameters and your on/off preference. These settings are stored with Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync, so they follow your Chrome profile if you use Chrome Sync. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

LinkScrub asks for access to the websites you visit only so it can do its one job on those pages. It does not read or record what you do there.
